# Wix migration audit

Source reviewed: `https://kumar1612.wixsite.com/mysite`

Reviewed on: 2026-08-09

## Migrated and strengthened

- Name, positioning, public email, LinkedIn, and Medium links.
- The credible 20+ year technology-leadership narrative.
- Anonymized FinTech and telecommunications experience.
- Education at Nanyang Technological University and the University of Madras.
- The themes of the three Wix posts, rewritten as concise leadership perspectives.
- The original abstract architectural visual, downloaded and served locally.

## Rebuilt

- The previously empty portfolio became the Engineering Intelligence Lab.
- Release Intelligence became a full case study with a live reference implementation.
- Navigation, responsive behavior, social sharing, search metadata, security headers,
  legacy-path redirects, and automated checks were added.

## Intentionally not migrated

- The Wix login, notification, and template chrome.
- Generic event listings with stale or inconsistent dates.
- The `Download CV` asset. The linked PDF was a Wix instructional placeholder CV,
  not a professional résumé.
- Generic stock imagery and unsupported client or employer claims.

## Content policy

Company-specific material remains anonymized. Public project data is synthetic.
No private employer information, client data, or confidential delivery metrics are
included.

## Cutover note

The free `wixsite.com` address belongs to Wix and cannot be transferred to Vercel.
A separately owned domain can be pointed to Vercel. The Wix site should remain live
until that domain resolves and the production deployment has been validated.
